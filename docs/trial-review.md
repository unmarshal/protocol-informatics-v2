# Reviewing a controlled trial pair

`review-trials <dir> <discovery-trial-id> <validation-trial-id>` returns a bounded, read-only evidence review. The two trials must have the `discovery` and `validation` roles and belong to one experiment. The response includes the experiment's question and prediction, capture and observation counts, five structural checks (fixed-width length prefix, length-adjusted chunks, typed length-value, unsigned LEB128, and delimiter), held-out format grouping, and suggested next actions. The same operation is available as the MCP `review_trials` tool and in the HTML report's **Trial evidence review** panel.

Each structural check reports whether it ran, its error if unavailable, total and reported candidate counts, the best held-out match count among reported candidates, up to three candidate configurations, and bounded counterexample observation IDs. `candidate_summaries_omitted` distinguishes the short review from the underlying inference result. Candidate lists may be truncated; `fully_validated_reported_count` only counts candidates actually returned by that method. `counterexample_ids_partial` warns when the underlying method omitted candidates or failures; the review then points to the full inference tool. The grouping check reports assigned, ambiguous, and unassigned held-out observations when at least one discovered group has three members. Otherwise it reports `insufficient_discovery_members` and suggests another controlled discovery capture. Read a named observation with `span` before making a field or grammar claim.

`next_actions` are evidence-based prompts with a tool hint. They call out missing discovery or validation captures, held-out model failures, multiple surviving structures, and grouping counterexamples. `capture_step` can execute a controlled acquisition using a stable key; `span` inspects exact bytes. A review does not run a capture, change hypothesis scores, or assess the experiment's natural-language prediction; `prediction_assessed` is therefore `false`. Hypothesis ratings and decisions are separate records. Re-run the review after new captures because its `revision` names the evidence set it examined.

The chunk check carries `identifiability`: counts of fully validated descriptions and distinct boundary rules, plus a `cross_length_width_boundary` capture suggestion when a shorter length field aliases a wider one. The review turns that threshold into a `next_action` for both discovery and validation captures. When several descriptions collapse to one boundary rule, it instead suggests `inspect_equivalent_descriptions`; changing only lengths cannot distinguish those descriptions. This still does not name the bytes or prove the grammar.
